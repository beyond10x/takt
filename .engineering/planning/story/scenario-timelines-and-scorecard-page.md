## Outcome

The site's Self-improvement section has, per scenario, the improvement timeline (waves, turns, free-arm medians split by model), and a scorecard page stating the promotion rule decided 2026-10-18 (design:recursive-self-improvement § Scorecard) and the latest `takt scorecard --against` result as a table (n, minimum, median and maximum per side, outcome ranks, verdict), plus one page per improvement turn from `docs/metrics/improvement-turns.jsonl`. All are generated by `takt-docs`.

## Context

From the owner's ask of 2026-10-19 (several reporters over one trace, the journey image on the public site, a report per run, more self-improvement on the site, the beyond10x docs style with a sidebar) and the read-only design written for it the same day. `docs/metrics/timeline.py` draws scenario 1 only (`timeline.py:23`), tokens and wall time only; nothing renders a saved scorecard; the one turn on the site is a hand-written table. `timeline.py` retires when the crate reproduces it.

## Scope

- `crates/takt-docs/`: timeline per scenario, scorecard and turn pages (inferred)
- `website/docs/improvement/` (generated) (inferred)
- `docs/metrics/timeline.py`: retired (cited)

## Acceptance

The generated timeline for scenario 1 has the same points as `docs/metrics/timeline.svg` at the commit that adds it; the scorecard page's table equals the output of `takt scorecard --against` on the saved baseline; there is one turn page per line of `improvement-turns.jsonl`.
