# Timeline

takt's own runs of scenario 1 per takt revision: tokens on the left axis, wall time where known on
the right, the waves that changed takt and the improvement turns as markers above the plot, and
the free-running medians as reference lines. `docs/metrics/timeline.py` draws it from
`scenario-runs.jsonl`, `improvement-turns.jsonl` and `waves.jsonl`, and this page inlines that
file at build time.

<Chart svg={timeline} caption="Scenario 1: takt runs to the merge approval, by takt revision. n per revision is in the rows under the axis. The chart keeps its size so its text stays readable; on a narrow screen, scroll it sideways." />

How to read it:

- Before wave 08 takt needed one or two resumes and was not timed; from 0a776d2 on it reached the
  merge approval in one invocation in most runs.
- Tokens did not fall after that. The promoted binary a0b8576 ran at a median of 122,361 tokens
  over 5 runs, against 122,438 at 0a776d2 over 3.
- The 56d1b70 column ran mostly on claude-fable-5-1 with no model pinned; its cost does not
  compare.
- The waves recorded so far are 06 to 11, merged on 2026-10-18 (`waves.jsonl`). A wave no measured
  revision contains yet gets an empty column at its merge commit.
