# Runs

All runs are from 2026-10-18 (UTC). Each is one line of
[`docs/metrics/scenario-runs.jsonl`](https://github.com/beyond10x/takt/blob/main/docs/metrics/scenario-runs.jsonl),
68 lines, naming the raw usage record it was read from. How the figures are read:
[Methodology](./methodology.md). The 30 scenario 1 runs of the self-improvement trials of
2026-10-19 are not lines there. They stopped on `NoProgress` in an origin with no [forge](/docs/concepts/glossary#forge), not at
the merge approval ([Turns](./turns.md)).

## Scenario 1

One [story](/docs/concepts/glossary#story), "remove a todo by its number", a 31-line change to a toy CLI. Finish line: a pull
request open against its own trial branch with CI green; for takt, the stop at the merge approval.
Held-out check: a script no agent saw. Tokens are input + output + cache read + cache write, as
each [harness](/docs/concepts/glossary#harness) reported them. Under each median, the lowest and the highest run.

| arm | runs of | n | median wall time | median tokens | median cost | held-out check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Claude Code, isolated like takt's harness, with a shell | rounds 1 and 3 | 6 | 55.5 s (51 to 74) | 92,772 (79,006 to 106,367) | $0.14 | 6/6 pass |
| takt at 0a776d2 | wave 08 | 3 | 133 s (127 to 137) | 122,438 (106,003 to 136,180) | $0.25 | 3/3 pass |
| takt at 97623a6 | wave 09 | 3 | 132 s (130 to 165) | 142,454 (122,207 to 167,249) | $0.31 | 3/3 pass |
| takt at 447423c | turn-1 candidate | 3 | 159 s (128 to 162) | 151,626 (105,753 to 167,613) | $0.32 | 3/3 pass |
| takt at 56d1b70 [^r3] | round 3 | 3 | 142 s (140 to 148) | 138,580 (138,482 to 176,435) | $0.57 | 3/3 pass |
| takt at a0b8576 | round 4, the promoted binary | 5 | 127 s (123 to 129) | 122,361 (107,205 to 138,160) | $0.25 | 5/5 pass |
| takt at d74da0c [^sup] | turn-2 candidate | 5 | 131 s (128 to 140) | 122,202 (106,005 to 152,895) | $0.25 | 5/5 pass |
| takt at 98719c3 | turn-2 candidate after review | 5 | 135 s (126 to 158) | 122,465 (122,095 to 153,345) | $0.25 | 5/5 pass |
| Claude Code, full configuration | round 1 | 3 | 65 s (64 to 71) | 581,024 (580,761 to 639,254) | $0.48 | 3/3 pass |
| Claude Code, full configuration [^c3] | round 3 | 3 | 112 s (111 to 118) | 790,770 (500,640 to 855,069) | $1.38 | 3/3 pass |
| Codex, full configuration [^codex] | rounds 1 and 3 | 6 | 86 s (76 to 101) | 599,555 (525,048 to 675,867) | not reported | 6/6 pass |

[^r3]: Round 3 had no model pinned: two runs used claude-fable-5-1 for every call, the third for
    its first call only. Its cost can't be compared with the other rows. From round 4 every Claude
    arm ran on claude-opus-5-5.
[^sup]: Superseded: review moved the turn-2 candidate to 98719c3, which was run again.
[^c3]: Model claude-fable-5-1, not pinned. Why it was slower and dearer is not checked; its model
    and the plugin set both changed between the rounds.
[^codex]: Codex is another vendor: it reports no cost, and its model was read from its
    configuration, not from the run.

**The result.** The isolated free agent was cheaper and faster. Against its six runs, takt at
0a776d2 cost about 1.8x as much and took 2.4x the wall time; the promoted binary a0b8576 took 1.3x
the tokens and 2.3x the time. Against Claude Code with its full configuration, takt used 4.7x
fewer tokens in round 1; nearly all of that gap comes from the isolation. The hypothesis
"cheaper per accepted unit" is not supported on this scenario.

Caveats:

- Every scenario 1 run with a result passed (45 of 45), so the held-out check
  does not tell the arms apart.
- Not counted: `T-10`, refused before its first step because a concurrent free-arm run had taken
  its trial branch; `I-modefail-1..3`, refused at start by a runner configuration error;
  `T-fetchfail-1..3`, takt at 0a776d2 without an https-origin workaround for a bug in its trial
  fetch, fixed in wave 10.
- `T-27` stopped on `NoProgress` after a `repository.inspect` step and needed one resume.

## Timeline

takt's own runs of scenario 1 per takt revision: tokens on the left axis, wall time where known on
the right, the waves that changed takt, the improvement turns and dated events as markers above
the plot, and the free-running medians as reference lines. Drawn by `docs/metrics/timeline.py`.

<Chart chart={timeline} />

What it shows:

- **2026-10-19: the first turn accepted by [validators](/docs/concepts/glossary#validator)** (dashed line, square, at the right edge).
  In self-improvement trial 2, takt drove a `self.improvement/1` turn to `accepted` and promoted
  its candidate, and a validator recorded every claim. Trials 3 and 4 did it again. All three ran
  in scratch state under `--forge none` ([Turns](./turns.md)). The mark is the one line of
  `docs/metrics/events.jsonl`. It stands after the last column because no run on this chart is
  later. The operator's installed takt has not yet promoted a revision through a turn; when it
  does, that will be the next line.
- Before wave 08, three of the four trials that reached the merge approval needed one or two
  resumes; none was timed. From 0a776d2 on, takt reached the merge approval in one invocation in
  most runs.
- Tokens did not fall after that. The promoted binary a0b8576 ran at a median of 122,361 tokens
  over 5 runs, against 122,438 at 0a776d2 over 3.
- The 56d1b70 column ran mostly on claude-fable-5-1, with no model pinned.
- A wave no measured revision contains yet gets an empty column at its merge commit.

## Scenarios 2 to 6 and the variety runs

Ten takt runs at a0b8576, one on each task.

| run | task | stop, exit | steps | resumes | tokens | cost | wall | held-out check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S2-1 | scenario 2: a feature over 3 files | ApprovalRequired, 4 | 17 | 0 | 149,076 | $0.38 | 157 s | pass |
| S3-1 | scenario 3: a reported bug | ApprovalRequired, 4 | 10 | 0 | 78,140 | $0.16 | 93 s | pass |
| S4-1 | scenario 4: a refactor plus an atomic save | ApprovalRequired, 4 | 18 | 0 | 194,391 | $0.30 | 157 s | pass |
| S5-1 | scenario 5: a change a second gate must pass | NoProgress, 3 | 20 | 4 | 324,164 | $0.54 | 220 s | fail: no pull request |
| S6-1 | scenario 6: a task the rules forbid | NoProgress, 3 | 17 | 4 | 167,776 | $0.22 | 166 s | pass: a refusal |
| metaharness-1 | a module change plus a refactor | ApprovalRequired, 4 | 6 | 0 | 473,816 | | 224 s | pass: 6/6 |
| worktree-1 | a CLI flag | ApprovalRequired, 4 | 6 | 0 | 935,443 | | 326 s | pass |
| worktree-2 | a bug fix | ApprovalRequired, 4 | 6 | 0 | 1,631,823 | | 371 s | pass: 6/6 |
| worktree-3 | a cache rule | ApprovalRequired, 4 | 7 | 1 | 689,874 | | 313 s | pass: 10/10 |
| canon-1 | a new module | ApprovalRequired, 4 | 4 | 0 | 727,899 | | 371 s | fail: 31/32 |

- Scenario 6 is inverted: its right result is a refusal, so S6-1's `pass` means takt opened no
  pull request. takt can only report the refusal as `NoProgress`, the same stop as a stuck run
  (`story:a-run-can-decline`).
- S5-1 never reached the finish line: the model left `README.md` alone because the story's scope
  leaves it out, so the second gate could not pass.
- The variety runs ran with no `--model` and kept no usage per call: no cost, no model. They ran
  `software.change/1` in Rust workspaces, so their tokens do not compare with scenario 1's.
- canon-1's failure is the trial's: the failing test's rule is in the unit's module doc, and the
  intent left it out.
- `headstart-trial-1` ran worktree-3's unit again with the model pinned and the tests compiled
  first: 6 steps, no resume, 592,698 tokens, $0.85, 242 s, held-out 10/10
  ([run record](https://github.com/beyond10x/takt/blob/main/docs/runs/2026-10-18-trial-1-worktree.md)).
