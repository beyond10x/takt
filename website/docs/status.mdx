# Status

- **Latest release:** 0.24.0, 2026-10-30 ([changelog](https://github.com/beyond10x/takt/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md), [install](./guides/install.md)).
- **Shipped** means a story is implemented and merged into `main`, which can be ahead of the latest release.
- **Open:** the stories of the open wave (Decided, [below](#every-story)), and the gaps on [Known limitations](./known-limitations.md).

## What 0.22.0 changes for you

| change | what you see | read |
|---|---|---|
| No wait for the next second | every evidence record's `observed_at` is written to the microsecond, and a record ordered after another one in the same second is stamped after it at once, where 0.21.0 waited up to 1.1 s | [Evidence and the judge](./concepts/evidence-and-the-judge.md) |
| `story.delivery/2` of engineering-protocols 0.14.0 | under `tests_first=required`, a red gate or CI admits the edit only after a test failed since the latest tests write, at its revision | [After a red gate](./guides/run-a-story.md#after-a-red-gate) |
| loom 0.50.0 with Canon 0.8.0 | Canon compares a case's records at the coarsest precision written among them; a case holding whole-second records is still compared in whole seconds | [Versions by release](./concepts/architecture.md#versions-by-release) |

## What 0.22.0 holds

The commands `run`, `approve`, `protocols`, `protocol`, `scenario`, `scorecard`, `trace`,
`report`, `view`, `promote` and `replay`, and prebuilt binaries for Linux (x86_64, aarch64) and
macOS (aarch64). Its built-in protocols are engineering-protocols 0.14.0's, `story.delivery/1`,
`story.delivery/2`, `software.change/1` and `self.improvement/1` among them, and takt's own
`story.delivery/3`; `takt protocols` lists them all
([Protocol and case](./concepts/protocol-and-case.md#the-protocols-takt-runs)). Its
[harnesses](./concepts/glossary.md#harness) are Claude Code (`--harness claude`, the default),
Codex (`codex`) and b10x-harness (`harness`), each through a metaharness session, or `none` to
run validators only.
